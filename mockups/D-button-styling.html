What changes (and why)

  • Nancy buttons: filled rose pink (#E91E63 — matches the article's brand color field), white text, bold, slightly larger, with → arrow, subtle shadow on hover.
  • Foil buttons: outlined gray, dark text, no arrow, slightly smaller — clearly the "alternative" option.
  • Urgency strips: unchanged. Still appear ONLY above Nancy buttons. This amplifies the asymmetry.
  • Result: by the time the reader sees product 3, their brain has pattern-matched "pink = recommended, gray = alternative." Conversion lift compounds with the urgency strips.
  • Mobile-safe: same button size, same line-height, same padding scale.
